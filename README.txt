PlayMate - created by Anish Angural

Open index.html in a browser (no build step needed).

index.html        page markup
css/style.css     all styles (colour tokens are at the top of :root)
js/app.js         venues data, pages, swappable card deck
fonts/            Marseille (letters only; its digits 0-4, rupee and star are placeholders in the free version)
images/           football hero photo

To use your own photos/PNGs for a venue, add images to the images folder and set
img:'images/yourfile.png' on that venue in the V list at the top of js/app.js.

Marseille is free for non-commercial use only.
